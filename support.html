How do I get to the Grown-Up Portal?

Tap Grown Up Portal in the top-right corner of the Home screen, then type the numbers that are spelled out on screen. This short grown-ups-only check keeps settings, purchases and the data tools out of little hands.

How does the free trial work?

The Magic OT Bug is free to download, and its games unlock with Magic Circle, a monthly or yearly membership. Magic Circle unlocks all games and Magic Quests, 2 Players, more than one child on the iPad, and the week at a glance and handwriting over time in the Grown-Up Portal.

It starts with a 14-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, one per Apple Account. While a trial runs, the Grown-Up Portal’s Home shows the day it ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don’t want to be charged. The price for your country is shown in the app before you buy.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On the iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose The Magic OT Bug, and tap Cancel Subscription. Members can also tap Manage on the plan card in Grown-Up Portal → Settings.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or free trial) to avoid the next charge. After cancelling, you keep Magic Circle until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPad?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you subscribed with. Magic Circle usually turns on by itself. If it does not, open the Magic Circle page and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of it: in Grown-Up Portal → Settings, tap the Join the Magic Circle card, or tap any locked game and type the numbers in the grown-up check. During first setup, the Magic Circle page at the end has the same button.

Once Magic Circle is on, Grown-Up Portal → Settings also shows Restore purchases under the plan card.

Can my family share one subscription?

Yes. Magic Circle supports Apple’s Family Sharing, so family members in your Family Sharing group can use it too.

On one iPad, Magic Circle also lets you add more than one child. Each child keeps their own progress, stickers and handwriting samples, and with 2 Players two children can take turns on the same level.

How do I erase my child’s data?

Everything your child does stays on your iPad. To erase every child’s data, open Grown-Up Portal → Settings → Privacy & safety → Delete all child data and tap Delete everything. App settings, such as sound, are kept.

To remove one child, open Grown-Up Portal → Settings → Children (or tap the child’s name at the top of the Grown-Up Portal), tap Edit beside that child, tap Delete at the top of their profile, and confirm. Deleting the app also removes its data from the iPad. A child who plays as Guest leaves nothing behind once the visit ends.

We keep no copy, so we cannot recover deleted data. An iCloud or computer backup made before you deleted may still include the app’s data until that backup is deleted or replaced.

What data do you collect?

None. The app collects no personal information from children or from anyone else. What it saves, such as each child’s profile, progress, stickers and creations, stays in the app’s own private storage on your iPad, and we cannot see it. There are no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking, no accounts and no chat. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

The Privacy Policy has the details.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app works offline. Buying or restoring Magic Circle goes through Apple’s App Store.

How do refunds and billing work?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or change your payment details.

We’re a clinic or school. How does it work for us?

Magic Circle is the same subscription for everyone. If you're setting it up for a clinic, a classroom or a school, please get in touch and we'll help.

What the app keeps about the children you work with stays on your iPad; the Privacy Policy has a section for therapists, teachers and schools.
