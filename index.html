Magic OT Bug Adventures LLC

We make The Magic OT Bug, an iPad app of playful games designed by pediatric occupational therapists. Children trace, pinch, copy, steer and match their way through activities that build the skills underneath handwriting: letter formation, fine motor, visual perception, coordination and following directions.

The app has no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and it collects no data. Everything a child does stays on the iPad.
